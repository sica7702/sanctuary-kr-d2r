[재검증 핵심]
1) Rare 공통: 2~6 random affixes, prefix <=3 / suffix <=3.
2) Rare amulet: 일반 FCR suffix 10%. 15~20% FCR amulet은 Caster Crafted 고정옵+접사 영역과 구분.
3) Circlet: Rare 20 FCR, 30 FRW, Mechanic's 1~2 sockets. 2/20/30/2os 계열 실제 2026 NL 시장 확인.
4) Gloves: Rare +2 skill tab / 20 IAS 핵심. +3 skill tab은 Magic 품질 영역.
5) Boots: 30 FRW + 10 FHR + high res 조합의 2026 Ladder/NL 수요 재확인. Strength cannot spawn as a normal rare-boots affix. GF/MF 특수 수요 별도.
6) Belt: 24 FHR 가능. Rare belt FCR로 오감정하지 않도록 Crafted와 분리.
7) Weapon: Cruel 201-300 ED, Fool's level-based AR/max, Quickness 40 IAS, Mechanic's 1-2 sockets, repair durability affixes 확인.
8) Amp: of Amplify Damage = 5% chance to cast lvl 1 Amplify Damage on striking, Weapons, cannot spawn on Orbs.
9) Class-specific: Druid Pelts and applicable Assassin Katars can carry up to 3 staffmods, each +1~+3. Claw +3 skill-tab is Magic-only; Rare can +1/+2 Assassin or +1/+2 skill-tab plus staffmods.
10) Thrown weapons: socket affix 제외. Replenish Quantity 별도 중요.

[시장 교차검증 예시]
- 2026 SC NL 30FRW/10FHR/tri-res boots ISO/FT 반복 확인.
- 2026 SC NL +2 Javelin/20IAS rare gloves + mana leech/dex/res 실제 ISO/매물 확인.
- 2026 SC NL 2Pal/10FCR + stats/res rare amulet 다수 거래/ISO 확인.
- 2026 SC NL 2Nec/20FCR/30FRW/2os circlet 및 2Barb/20FCR/30FRW/2os 수요 확인.
- 2026 Amp bow ISO에서 Amp + IAS + high ED, 일부 2os/베이스 조건 확인.
